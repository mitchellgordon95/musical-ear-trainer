Three exercises on hooks you know by heart, all against a tonal centre. 1 · Find the tonic: the hook plays; find home on the little keyboard. 2 · Name the degrees: the song is shown; name every note of the hook as a scale degree over the tonic drone, staying on a note until you get it. 3 · Name the song: the degrees are shown and the title is hidden; hear it in your head and say which song it is. The real clip plays after each answer. No singing required.
